Popular Travel Destinations

Discover three amazing places to visit in India.

Goa

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches, water sports, Portuguese architecture and lively nightlife.

Best time: November to February

Famous for: Beaches, seafood and forts

Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station surrounded by mountains, green valleys, rivers and snow-covered landscapes.

Best time: October to June

Famous for: Snowfall, trekking and adventure

Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City, is known for royal palaces, historic forts, colourful markets and Rajasthani culture.

Best time: October to March

Famous for: Forts, palaces and culture
